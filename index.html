Who I am

I grew up in India and did my Master's in Computer Science at Indiana State University. Today I'm an AI Solutions Architect at MAQ Software, where the work spans the whole range: writing the code, designing the system, leading the project, and standing in front of the customer to show it working.

Most of what I build is around making AI useful beyond the demo. I'm drawn to systems that have to reason, interact with other systems, and make decisions in the real world, and to the engineering underneath that makes those systems possible. I like taking new technology apart, understanding where it breaks, and figuring out how to turn it into something dependable without losing what made it interesting in the first place.

I move between roles because I've had to: hands-on engineer, solutions architect, project lead. Some days I'm deep in the code; other days I'm thinking about architecture, working through a problem with a customer, or helping a team decide what to build next. I care most about the decisions that aren't always visible: the boundaries, tradeoffs, and small details that determine whether a system actually holds up.
